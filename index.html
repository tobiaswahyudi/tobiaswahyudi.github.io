Games

I really enjoy making browser games, probably because they remind me of flash games. Most of these are engine-less and even library-less, developed with vanilla JS on Canvas API.

Gobbo Slayor

Jun 2025 - Mar 2026

Sokoban-like (?) puzzle game. You must clear all goblins, but each hit changes the shape and position of your aiming. It was really fun to see how complex we could make the levels, while not expanding the simple ruleset.

Collaborative project with my siblings Terri and Thalia. Developed and illustrated by me. Designed and puzzleset together.

Community acclaimed.

Ouroboros Wrangler

Aug 2025

Sokoban-like puzzle game. You control the wrangler, and the snake moves deterministically. Guide the snake to eat apples and ultimately bite its own tail. Inspired by Paquerette down the Bunburrows for puzzle reasoning around deterministic NPCs.

Collaborative project with my siblings Terri and Thalia. Created for GMTK Game Jam 2025 with the theme "Loop".

New Media Art / Studies

All of these projects are written in p5js.

I so deeply love p5js. I find it very very comfortable. It's almost always the first thing I pick up to sketch out an idea, or sometimes I'll use it as a visual complement to jupyter notebooks.

I also love its mission of accessibility & teaching, but it's also powerful enough that I've seen more than a few art installations use it.

When any friend expresses a desire to start learning programming, I literally always tell them to pick up p5js.
